Reactor Networks - add units from palette and connect with pipes

UNDER CONSTRUCTION. This works with keyboards only: Hold down option/Alt key and click a unit in the palette on the left, then click on the scene to the right to add the unit to the scene. To remove a unit, option/Alt click on the unit. You can add and remove multiple units.

To add a "pipe" connecting two units, option/Alt click on a blue output in one of the units in the scene, then simple-click on a yellow input in another unit. Option/Alt click on a yellow input to remove a pipe.

Click on the small black square in the lower-left corner of a unit to change its reaction parameters. During a run, concentration (c) and flowrate (f) are displayed in each unit.

Click the Run button to start.
